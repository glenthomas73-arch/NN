Who We Are

The Ultimate
Feel-Good Duo

N30N N0iZE are a high-energy party duo bringing the perfect blend of style, charisma, and non-stop party vibes to every event. With Michelle's powerful vocals leading the way and Glen's dynamic guitar and backing vocals driving the sound, they deliver a mix everyone loves.

From crowd-favourite hits and sing-along classics to the latest modern anthems, every set is crafted to keep the dance floor packed from the very first song to the very last call.

Based in Liverpool and available across the Northwest UK — perfect for weddings, parties, corporate events and live venues. Michelle & Glen guarantee a night full of music, energy, and memories that last a lifetime.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much do you cost?
Price on application — every event is different, so pricing depends on your date, location and event type. Get in touch with your details and we'll send you a tailored quote.
How long do you play for?
We're flexible — 2 x 45 minutes, 2 x 60 minutes, or 3 x 30 minutes, with music between sets to keep the dance floor moving all night. Price on application depending on the format you choose.
Do you bring your own sound and lighting?
Yes — we bring our own full PA system and lighting rig, so there's nothing extra for you to arrange or hire.
Can we request songs?
Absolutely. Send us your must-plays (and do-not-plays) in advance and we'll work them into the set.
Where do you travel to?
We're based in Liverpool and cover the Northwest — including Manchester, Cheshire and Lancashire. Further afield? Just ask.
How do we book you?
Fill in the enquiry form below or email us your date — we'll confirm availability and get back to you quickly. A deposit secures your booking.
